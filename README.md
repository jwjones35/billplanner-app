# Bill Planner

See what's due, what's coming in, and what's available to spend until your next payday.

Bill Planner is a web app you add to your phone's Home Screen. It has no accounts and no servers: everything you enter stays on your device.

- Website: https://jwjones35.github.io/billplanner-app/
- The app: https://jwjones35.github.io/billplanner-app/app/

## What it does

- Bills and paydays in one place, with **Available to spend** until your next payday.
- A warning when next week's bills are bigger than next week's pay.
- Past-due bills, autopay bills that haven't gone through yet, and payments the bank hasn't shown yet.
- One-time bills, bills that change each month, and transaction fees.
- Money set aside for something specific, and savings tips when there's room to save.
- Bill reminders in your phone's calendar.
- Backup files you keep yourself.
- Optional: connect your bank through [SimpleFIN Bridge](https://beta-bridge.simplefin.org/) (a separate paid service) so balances and paid bills update by themselves.

## Add it to your phone

- **iPhone:** open the app's link in Safari, tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open the link in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

## Privacy

Nothing you enter is sent anywhere. There's no tracking or advertising. If you connect your bank, the app reads your balances and transactions from SimpleFIN directly from your device.

## Important

Bill Planner is a planning tool, not financial advice. Its numbers are only as good as what's entered and what your bank reports. Make a backup now and then: deleting the app or clearing your browser's data erases what's in it.
